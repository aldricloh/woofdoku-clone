Woofdoku

One dog per row, column and colour. Dogs never touch, not even corner to corner.

How to play

Find every dog its own patch of the park.

One per row, column and colourEach row, each column and each coloured patch holds exactly one dog.
Dogs need spaceNo two dogs may touch, not even diagonally. Their 8 neighbours stay empty.

Tap to markTap a square once for an X (no dog here), again for a dog, again to clear. Drag across squares to X many at once.
